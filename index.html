📘 Game rules

Roll the animated six-sided die. Your pawn travels clockwise around 24 colorful category spaces; the space you land on determines your question. Answer within 25 seconds. A correct answer scores 100 points and makes progress toward that category’s wedge. In Quick mode, two correct answers in a category earn its wedge; Classic mode requires a correct answer on a gold landmark space for the wedge. Turns alternate after each question. The first player to collect all six wedges wins. There are 150 questions in every category, with no repeat in a match.

Gold-outline spaces are wedge landmarks. They grant +50 bonus points when you answer correctly there; collecting wedges still takes two correct answers in that category, wherever you land. If all 900 questions are used, most wedges wins, with points as the tiebreaker.

Two-phone multiplayer uses native browser WebRTC and manual invitation/response exchange. No additional hosting or room-signaling account is needed. Connectivity may fail on restrictive networks without a TURN relay. Codes may expose network-address information, so share them privately. Keep the host tab open.
