How do the rules work?

Choose a category on your turn, then pick one of four answers within 25 seconds. Correct answers earn 100 points and fill one of two progress dots in that category. Two correct answers earn its colored wedge. Turns alternate after every question. First player to collect all six wedges wins; if the game runs out of questions, the most wedges wins (points break ties).

Online mode uses a live peer-to-peer connection. Both phones must have this page open with an internet connection; the room host must stay connected. No account is required. Online mode uses the PeerJS public signaling service. For reliable play, host this page on an HTTPS website.
